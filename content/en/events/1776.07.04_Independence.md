The Declaration of Independence was the first formal statement by a nation's people asserting their right to choose their own government.

## Key Points

- Written primarily by Thomas Jefferson
- Adopted by the Continental Congress on July 4, 1776
- Announced the separation of thirteen North American British colonies from Great Britain
- Influenced by Enlightenment philosophy and the concept of natural rights

![The Founding Documents](declaration.svg)

The parchment above is the embodiment of the colonists' grievances and aspirations — a single document that reshaped the future of governance.

## Impact

The Declaration has influenced countless independence movements around the world and remains a foundational document of American democracy.
