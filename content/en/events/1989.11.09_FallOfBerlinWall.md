The fall of the Berlin Wall was a pivotal event in world history that marked the beginning of the end of the Cold War.

## Background

- The Berlin Wall had divided East and West Berlin since 1961
- It symbolized the Iron Curtain between communist and democratic nations
- Thousands had died trying to cross it

## The Night of November 9

- East German government announced new travel regulations
- Thousands of East Berliners gathered at the wall
- Border guards opened the gates after confusion about the new rules
- People from both sides celebrated together

![Celebrations beside the Wall](berlin-wall.svg)

For 28 years the Wall stood as a scar across Berlin. On this night, chipped concrete and torn ironwork became souvenirs of freedom.

## Consequences

- Led to German reunification in 1990
- Symbolized the collapse of communist regimes across Eastern Europe
- Marked the beginning of a new era in international relations
