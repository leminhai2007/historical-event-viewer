# Alaska Purchase
On March 30, 1867, the United States purchased Alaska from the Russian Empire for $7.2 million.
## Background

  - Negotiated by Secretary of State William Seward
  - Widely mocked as "Seward's Folly"

> Seward's icebox, as detractors called it.
It added more than 586,000 square miles of territory to the United States.
