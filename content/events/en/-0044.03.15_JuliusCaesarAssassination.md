Julius Caesar, dictator of Rome, was assassinated by a group of senators who
feared he was becoming a king.

## The Ides of March

- Caesar was stabbed 23 times at a Senate meeting on March 15, 44 BCE.
- The conspirators were led by Brutus and Cassius.
- Caesar's death triggered a civil war that ended the Roman Republic.
