Wright Brothers First Flight

Orville and Wilbur Wright made the first powered, sustained, controlled airplane flight at Kitty Hawk, North Carolina on December 17, 1903.

Highlights:
- 12 seconds aloft, covering 120 feet
- Three more flights that day, the longest 59 seconds
