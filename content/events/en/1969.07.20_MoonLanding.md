Apollo 11 was the spaceflight that first landed humans on the Moon.

## The Mission

- Launched from Kennedy Space Center on July 16, 1969
- Crew: Neil Armstrong (commander), Buzz Aldrin (lunar module pilot), Michael Collins (command module pilot)
- Armstrong and Aldrin landed the Lunar Module "Eagle" on July 20

![Apollo 11 lifting off](moon-landing.svg)

The Saturn V rocket carried 363 feet of raw power above the Florida coast, propelling humanity beyond its planetary cradle.

## "One Small Step"

Neil Armstrong became the first human to walk on the Moon, followed by Buzz Aldrin about 19 minutes later.

> "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind."

## Legacy

- Fulfilled President Kennedy's goal of landing on the Moon before the end of the 1960s
- Demonstrated human capability for space exploration
- Inspired generations of scientists and engineers
